Controls & Navigation

  • WASD or arrow keys to move.
  • Mouse Look: Smooth first-person camera rotation.

Keys, Gates & Switches

  • Collect keys (Amber, Magenta, Green, Cyan) for matching gates.
  • Touch stone Switch Pedestals to rotate levers and open barricades.
  • Search for Ancient Relics hidden in secret chambers!
⚡

Pressure Plates & Traps

  • Step on carved Pressure Plates to depress mechanisms.
  • Beware of Timed Doors that tick down before locking again!
  • Dimensional Teleporters instantly warp across impassable voids.
  • Ignite Checkpoint Obelisks to save your safe respawn point.

Credits & Computer Graphics Techniques

Demonstrating advanced real-time Three.js WebGL graphics architectures integrated naturally into gameplay.

GPU Optimization

InstancedMesh Wall Batching

Batches hundreds of maze walls and pillars into a single GPU draw call using THREE.InstancedMesh with 4×4 transform matrices, achieving 60+ FPS on student laptops.

Procedural Texturing

Mathematical Canvas Shaders

Generates 5 distinct procedural materials (Sunstone runes, glacial ice crystals, molten magma veins, and obsidian bricks) dynamically in memory with zero image downloads.

Lighting & Shadows

PCF Soft Shadow Mapping

Dynamic directional light frustum fitted tightly to maze bounds with Percentage-Closer Filtering (PCF) for smooth, realistic architectural penumbras.

VFX & Particles

Atmospheric Particle Systems

World-tailored GPU particle emitters simulating floating ancient dust motes, falling crystalline snowflakes, rising molten embers, and swirling void motes.

Audio Engineering

Procedural Web Audio Synth

Real-time Web Audio API multi-oscillator synthesis for footstep clicks, key chimes, stone grinding doors, and guardian victory fanfares without external audio assets.

Graph Theory

Reachability & Path Solvers

Breadth-First Search (BFS) graph reachability validation ensuring every key, door, switch, and exit portal is 100% solvable before a chamber begins.
