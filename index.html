How to Play

Movement & Navigation

Use WASD or arrow keys to move. Move the mouse to rotate the first-person perspective camera. Press P or Esc to pause.

Keys & Sealed Doors

Doors are sealed by ancient locks. Walk over a glowing Amber, Magenta, or Green key to collect it, then approach its matching door to unlock it.

Patrolling Sentinels

Ancient sentinels patrol the corridors. If a sentinel touches you, you will be repelled and suffer a +10s time penalty. Listen for the sentinel hum!

Radiant Exit Beacon

A towering beam of golden light marks the exit portal. Spot it down long corridors and through the mist to guide your escape.
